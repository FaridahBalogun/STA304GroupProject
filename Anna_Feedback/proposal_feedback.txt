Rating scales
Your questionnaire uses a 1 to 5 scale from strongly agree to strongly disagree. Please add "slightly agree" and "slightly disagree" to make it a 1 to 7 scale. Much of your proposed analysis treats these responses as quantitative data, and more points on the scale give you more variation and better results.

Commute time
Commute time is not a sensitive question, so a range does not protect anyone. Please ask students to enter their commute time as a number, in minutes or hours, whichever you prefer. Ranges force you to use the midpoint of each interval in your analysis, and exact values give you more to work with.
